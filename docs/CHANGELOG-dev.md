# 🥛 MME Extensions v1.6.0 dev Changelog

## 🌟 Major Features

- 👙 **Individual Armor Modifications:** Erotic upgrades are now tracked per armor item instead of by armor name like the original MME. Every outfit remembers its own naughty little secrets!

## 🔧 Minor Features

- 🐄 **[Shadowman's Breastfeeding Shout](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/90938):** Added milk-drinking effects and optional Skyrim.Net reactions. Unleash the mighty voice of the dairy!
- 📣 **Milk Maid Shout Learning:** Player Milk Maids can automatically learn the Breastfeeding Shout, with a toggle enabled by default. Your thirst has found its voice!
- 👏 **NPC Movement Sounds:** Nearby Milk Maids now slap and flop too, with their own toggle and volume control. Skyrim's dairy orchestra has expanded!
- 🍼 **Consensual Breastfeeding:** AI Milk Maids now ask for consent before breastfeeding. No more surprise milk deliveries!
- 🌐 **MCM Translations:** Added Chinese and Russian translations, selectable during FOMOD installation. Skyrim's dairy business goes international!

## ✨ Improvements

- 👙 **Milk Maid Stories:** Stories now trigger only when equipping new milking armor or after 8 hours. The same old outfit shouldn't surprise your girls every time they re-equip it!
- ✏️ **Magelight UI Label Editing:** Click the pen button to rename labels directly in the editor. Make it your own!
- 🥛 **Milk Maid Prompt:** Improved Skyrim.Net prompts so Milk Maids more reliably recognize their own status. No more identity crises at the dairy!
- 🤖 **Natural Skyrim.Net Decorators:** Improved Milk Maid descriptions to use natural language instead of code-like data. Now the AI understands your girls like characters, not spreadsheets!
- 🪄 **Magelight UI Editor:** Improved grammar, descriptions, and terminology throughout the editor. Less technical jargon, more natural wording, and a friendlier experience!
- 📚 **Skyrim.Net Milk Maid Knowledge Pack:** Moved Milk Maid lore into a dedicated knowledge pack, helping AI NPCs understand what Milk Maids are. Now everyone *might* finally know where Skyrim's milk comes from!

## 🐛 Bug Fixes

- 🛑 **Movement Sound Cleanup:** Breast slaps and flops now start and stop more responsively with movement. The girls finally know when to stop applauding!
- 💗 **Arousal Framework Checks:** MME Extensions now pauses repeated arousal checks when required information is missing. No more endlessly asking a confused framework for attention!
- 👗 **Naked Orgasm Armor Checks:** Fixed invalid armor checks during orgasms when naked, while normal milking continues. No need to inspect an outfit that isn't there!
- 🐄 **Elsie LaVache Detection:** Compatibility checks now quietly skip when Elsie LaVache isn't installed. No more searching for missing milkmaids!
